Q&A

Your questions,
my answers.

A few things clients usually ask before starting a project.

What services do you offer?+

UI/UX design in Figma, wireframing and prototyping, plus responsive frontend development — from landing pages to full product dashboards.

Do you only design, or also build the frontend?+

Both. Every project is designed in Figma first, then hand-built as a responsive frontend — so the design and the live product always match.

How long does a typical project take?+

It depends on scope — a landing page moves faster than a full product dashboard. Timelines are confirmed once the requirements are clear.

Do you offer revisions?+

Yes. Feedback is part of the process, and revisions are included to make sure the final result matches your expectations.

How can I start a project or get a quote?+
